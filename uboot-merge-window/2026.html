U-Boot Concepts

  • Twilight Time: Patches whose V1 was submitted during the merge window remain eligible even if revised versions come during stabilization.
  • Custodians: Subsystem maintainers who collect and forward patches to the main maintainer.
  • Meetings: Community meetings held the day after each RC/release (Tuesdays, 9 AM CST).
For meeting time & Google Meet link, see Tom Rini's [ANN] U-Boot vXXXX.XX-rcX released emails on the u-boot mailing list.
